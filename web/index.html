    p. 6 · §3.2 Method

    Scaled Dot-Product Attention

    We compute attention as softmax(QKT/√dk)V. For large values of dk, the dot products grow large in magnitude, pushing the softmax into regions with extremely small gradients[4]. To counteract this, we scale by 1/√dk.
    Q · why do they grow?
    If q and k have unit-variance entries, q·k has variance dk, so magnitude scales with √dk.
    Q · what is [4]?
    Hochreiter et al. on vanishing gradients.
